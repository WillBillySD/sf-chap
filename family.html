Frequently Asked Questions

Can we bring our own decorations?

Absolutely! We encourage you to personalize the space with family photos, decorations, and meaningful touches. Our team can assist with setup and will provide guidelines for what's allowed.

Is the venue accessible for elderly family members and those with mobility challenges?

Yes, The Chapel is fully ADA-compliant with elevator access, accessible restrooms, ramps, and wide doorways. We're happy to discuss any specific accessibility needs.

Can we bring children? Are there activities for kids?

Children are absolutely welcome! We have a designated children's activity area and can help arrange age-appropriate entertainment, games, and supervision if needed.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions and food allergies?

Yes, our catering team can accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, kosher, halal, and other dietary needs. Just let us know your family's requirements when planning.

How many overnight guest rooms do you have?

We have 6 on-site guest rooms available on a first-come basis. For larger groups, we partner with nearby hotels for discounted room blocks.

Can we play our own music or hire a DJ?

Yes! You're welcome to use our sound system with your own playlists, or we can recommend professional DJs and live musicians who are familiar with our venue.

A Legacy of Celebration

For over a century, The Chapel has stood as a landmark in downtown Sioux Falls, bearing witness to countless love stories, family milestones, and community gatherings. Today, we honor that legacy while creating new memories for generations to come.

Our Story

Historic Beginnings

Built in the early 1900s, The Chapel originally served as a place of worship and community gathering for Sioux Falls residents. Its beautiful architecture, featuring soaring ceilings, stained glass windows, and intricate woodwork, reflected the craftsmanship and artistry of the era.

Thoughtful Restoration

After years of careful restoration, we've preserved the building's historic character while incorporating modern amenities. Every detail—from the restored original floors to the state-of-the-art climate control—has been thoughtfully chosen to honor the past while serving today's needs.

A New Chapter

Today, The Chapel continues its legacy as a gathering place, now serving as Sioux Falls' premier event venue. We're proud to host weddings, corporate events, and family celebrations, creating new memories in a space steeped in history.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long has The Chapel been operating as an event venue?

While the building itself dates back to the early 1900s, we've been operating as a premier event venue since completing our extensive restoration. We've hosted hundreds of successful weddings, corporate events, and family celebrations.

What makes The Chapel different from other venues?

Our combination of historic elegance, modern amenities, exceptional culinary offerings, and personalized service sets us apart. We're not just a beautiful space—we're a full-service venue with an experienced team dedicated to making your event perfect.

Can we tour The Chapel before booking?

Absolutely! We encourage tours so you can experience our spaces in person, meet our team, and envision your event. Schedule a tour at your convenience.

Do you only host certain types of events?

While we specialize in weddings, corporate events, and family gatherings, we're equipped to host a wide variety of celebrations. Contact us to discuss your specific event needs—we're flexible and creative in adapting our spaces.

How far in advance should we book?

Popular dates (especially weekend weddings in peak season) often book 12-18 months in advance. However, we sometimes have availability for shorter timelines, especially for weekday events or off-peak seasons. Contact us to check availability for your preferred date.

What's included in your event coordination services?

Our coordination services include initial planning consultations, vendor recommendations and coordination, timeline development, setup oversight, day-of event management, and problem-solving support. We're with you every step of the way.

Can you accommodate guests with special needs or accessibility requirements?

Yes, The Chapel is fully ADA-compliant with accessible entrances, elevators, restrooms, and parking. We can also accommodate dietary restrictions, sensory sensitivities, and other special needs. Just let us know what you require.

Do you offer tasting appointments for catering?

Yes! For wedding packages and large events, we offer tasting appointments where you can sample menu options and work with our culinary team to customize your selections. Tastings are scheduled as part of the planning process.

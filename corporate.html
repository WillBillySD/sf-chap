Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the rental fee?

All packages include room rental, standard AV equipment, Wi-Fi, tables and chairs, basic setup/breakdown, and coordination support. Catering, premium AV, and additional services can be added.

Can we bring our own AV equipment or vendors?

You're welcome to use our in-house AV systems, which are professional-grade and fully supported. If you need specialized equipment, we can coordinate with approved AV vendors.

Is parking available for attendees?

Yes, we have a private parking lot with space for 50+ vehicles, plus additional street parking nearby. We can arrange overflow parking for larger events.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Our catering team can accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, kosher, halal, and other dietary requirements. Just let us know your attendees' needs.

Can we do a site visit before booking?

We encourage site visits! Schedule a tour to see our spaces, test our technology, meet our team, and discuss your specific event needs.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellation policies vary by package and timing. We work with clients to find flexible solutions when plans change. Details are provided in your event contract.

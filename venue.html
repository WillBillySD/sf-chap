Venue Questions

What's the maximum capacity?

The Chapel can accommodate up to 200 guests for ceremonies and 150 for seated receptions. We also host smaller, more intimate gatherings with minimums varying by season and day of the week.

Do you allow outside vendors?

We provide exceptional in-house catering and have preferred vendor partnerships for other services. Outside vendors may be considered on a case-by-case basis with prior approval.

Is the venue climate-controlled year-round?

Yes, The Chapel features modern HVAC systems ensuring comfortable temperatures in all seasons. Both heating and air conditioning maintain ideal conditions for guests.

What's included with venue rental?

All packages include event spaces, tables, chairs, linens, basic AV equipment, setup and breakdown, and event coordination. Catering, premium AV, and other services can be added.

Can we customize the space with our own decorations?

Absolutely! We encourage personalizing the space while protecting our historic features. Our team will guide you on what's permitted and can recommend decoration vendors.

Is there a rain plan for outdoor events?

Yes, our flexible indoor spaces serve as beautiful backup options if weather doesn't cooperate. We monitor forecasts and work with you to make seamless transitions if needed.

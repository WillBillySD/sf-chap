Contact FAQs

Do I need an appointment to visit The Chapel?

While walk-ins are welcome during business hours, we highly recommend scheduling a tour so we can give you our full attention, show you all our spaces, and answer your questions thoroughly.

How quickly can I book an event?

We can accommodate events on relatively short notice depending on availability. For weddings, we recommend booking 12-18 months in advance. Corporate events and family gatherings may have more flexible timelines.

What happens after I submit a form?

Our event team will review your information and respond within one business day via email or phone. For tour requests, we'll confirm your preferred date/time or suggest alternatives. For event quotes, we'll schedule a consultation to discuss your needs in detail.

Can I visit The Chapel if I'm from out of town?

Absolutely! Many of our clients are planning destination events. We offer flexible tour times and can also arrange virtual tours via video call if you can't visit in person before booking.

Is there a fee for tours or consultations?

No, tours and initial event consultations are always complimentary. We want you to experience The Chapel and learn about our services without any obligation.

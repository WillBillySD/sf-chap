Booking Information

How do I book The Chapel?

Start by scheduling a tour to see our spaces. After your visit, we'll provide a customized proposal. To secure your date, we require a signed contract and deposit (typically 25-50% of the total).

What's included in the starting prices?

Starting prices include venue rental, standard furniture and linens, basic AV equipment, event coordination, and setup/breakdown. Catering, premium services, and add-ons are priced separately based on your selections.

Are there minimums or additional fees?

Minimum spends vary by season, day of week, and package selected. Saturday weddings typically have higher minimums than weekday corporate events. We'll provide transparent pricing with no hidden fees in your proposal.

What's your payment schedule?

We typically require a deposit to secure your date, with the remaining balance due 30 days before your event. Payment plans can be arranged for events booked far in advance.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellation policies are detailed in your event contract and vary by package and timing. We work with clients to find fair solutions when circumstances change.

Do you offer discounts?

We offer discounts for off-peak dates, weekday events, military families, nonprofit organizations, and returning clients. Ask about current promotions when you contact us.

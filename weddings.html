Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking 12-18 months in advance, especially for popular dates (May-October weekends). However, we often have availability for shorter timelines and can accommodate last-minute weddings when possible.

Do you provide catering, or can we bring our own?

We offer exceptional in-house catering with customizable menus. While we prefer to provide catering to ensure quality and coordination, we can discuss outside caterers on a case-by-case basis.

What's included in the venue rental?

All packages include access to ceremony and reception spaces, tables, chairs, basic linens, sound system, event coordination, and setup/breakdown. Premium packages include enhanced amenities and extended access.

Is there parking available?

Yes, we have a private parking lot adjacent to the venue, plus additional street parking nearby. We can also arrange valet service for an additional fee.

Can we have our rehearsal dinner at The Chapel?

Absolutely! We can accommodate rehearsal dinners and day-after brunches. These can be added to your wedding package or booked separately.

Do you offer lodging for out-of-town guests?

We have a limited number of guest rooms available on-site, perfect for the wedding party or family members. We also partner with nearby hotels for discounted room blocks.

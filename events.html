Why Choose The Chapel?

Historic Elegance

Our beautifully restored historic building provides a unique and memorable setting that combines old-world charm with contemporary comfort and convenience.

Full-Service Experience

From initial planning to event day coordination, our experienced team handles every detail so you can focus on enjoying your special occasion with your guests.

Flexible Spaces

Multiple indoor and outdoor venues can be configured to suit events of any size or style, from intimate gatherings to grand celebrations.

Prime Location

Located in the heart of downtown Sioux Falls at 610 S Dakota Ave, we're easily accessible and close to hotels, restaurants, and local attractions.

Exceptional Catering

Our culinary team creates custom menus featuring locally-sourced ingredients, with options for every dietary need and preference.

Modern Amenities

State-of-the-art AV equipment, high-speed Wi-Fi, climate control, and accessible facilities ensure comfort and convenience for all guests.

Planning Your Event

Our event planning process is designed to be simple and stress-free:

  1. Schedule a Tour: Visit The Chapel to see our spaces and envision your event
  2. Consultation: Meet with our event team to discuss your vision, needs, and budget
  3. Custom Proposal: Receive a detailed proposal with package options and pricing
  4. Book Your Date: Secure your preferred date with a deposit
  5. Planning Support: Work with our coordinators to finalize all details
  6. Event Day: Relax while our team ensures everything runs perfectly
